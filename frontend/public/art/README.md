## Prompt set

1. **Temple sanctuary.** Exquisite wide Vedic website illustration inspired by the supplied reference: antique devotional painting, sepia copper engraving, golden parchment grain. A sandstone temple with domed chhatris and steps beside a sacred lotus lake on the right; a sun mandala, peacock at lower right, swans, white and pink lotuses, marigolds and a turtle. Keep the left as open cream parchment for website copy. Realistic painterly detail. No UI, text, logos, modern neon or vector styling.
2. **Rishi garden.** Wide antique devotional painting on cream parchment, sepia fine engraving, saffron, muted rose and gold. Realistic elderly rishi with white beard and saffron robes on the left beside a square yajna altar with glowing fire; ancient carved temple columns and a banyan tree. On the right, a tranquil pond with two swans, white and pink lotuses, a turtle, marigolds and a peacock feather. Crescent moon and distant hills, grain and detailed ink. No text, vector shapes or neon.
3. **Graha folio.** Wide 3:1 antique celestial atlas. Six equally spaced planetary medallions in one row on ivory parchment: realistic fiery Sun, cratered Moon, clouded cream Venus, rusty Mars, striped Jupiter with a spot, and Saturn with rings. Sepia and gold copperplate detail with astrolabe markings. No text, neon, black background or vector styling.
